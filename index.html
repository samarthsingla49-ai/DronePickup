📖 NEO-TOKYO COURIER // PILOT CODEX & RULES

// THE STORY //

In 2077, Neo-Tokyo's ground avenues are fractured by acid storms and megacorp blockades. As an autonomous Courier Pilot, you are the skyline's only lifeline. Fly through restricted airspace, latch high-value payloads, and deliver them safely to rooftop receptor pads.

🕹️
1. FLIGHT & TILT

W / ↑ fires main thrusters. A / D or ← / → tilts the chassis. Conserve battery with gentle bursts.

🧲
2. TETHER & WINCH

SPACE latches or releases cargo. Press Q to Retract (shortens cable to stop swing), press E to Extend (smooth landings).

📦
3. CARGO DELIVERY

Fly cargo into the green rooftop pad. Touch down gently (< 40 px/s). Release with SPACE or settle for 0.5s to score!

⚡
4. RECHARGE STATIONS

Low power? Land or hover low over green ⚡ RECHARGE rooftop pads to restore 100% battery in 2 seconds.

⚠️
5. HAZARD MATRIX

Avoid orbital security scan lasers (red beams). Flying above altitude y < 60 triggers turbulent storm ceilings.

★
6. SCORING & GRADES

Earn credits based on Cargo Value + Time Left + Cargo Integrity + Battery Left. High ratios award S+ / S / A grades!
